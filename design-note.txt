The IDR logo employs a cube-inspired geometric shape to symbolize robust structure, foundational resilience, and the multi-faceted nature of digital risk. The primary color palette of vibrant orange, stark white, and deep black/dark gray creates a high-contrast, modern aesthetic that feels both tech-forward and academic. Clean, unornamented typography further emphasizes clarity and professional gravity, ensuring the brand mark is striking and legible across any screen size.
